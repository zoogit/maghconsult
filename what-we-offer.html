What We Offer

Maghame IP Consulting can partner with you strategically to navigate the complexities of a wide variety of intellectual property, transactional and policy matters. Our expertise in licensing, litigation and standards-related issues globally allows us to serve as an added resource for your in-house team and provide legal and advisory services as needed. With access to a range of specialists to efficiently manage projects and advise on legal and technical matters, we can help you achieve your business goals.

IP Strategy & Portfolio Management

Crafting robust IP strategy, including portfolio development and management, to protect and optimize the value of innovations.

IP Licensing

Negotiating, managing, and supporting IP licensing deals to achieve positive results consistent with company strategy.

Standards and Standard-Essential Patents

Advising on matters relating to standards and standard-essential patents, including competition law and regulatory issues.

Litigation Management & Support

Providing support on IP and commercial litigation matters from start to finish, including settlement negotiations and expert witness services.

Start-ups and SMEs

Advising start-ups and small and medium enterprises on IP matters, including protection, monetization, regulatory, M&A, and related issues.

Patents and IPR Policy

Consulting on regulatory initiatives and policy matters affecting company IP and competitive position globally.

Commercial Transactions

Negotiating, supporting, and documenting complex commercial transactions, including M&A, patent acquisitions and divestitures, and other business deals.
