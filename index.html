Legal and IP-related Services

Maghame IP Legal Consulting provide clients with a wide range of legal, strategic counseling and expert services on licensing, litigation, transactional and regulatory matters. With a proven track record of success in navigating the complex landscape of technology standards development and standard essential patents (SEPs), we assist our clients, industry groups and government regulators in addressing these issues.

We offer a deep understanding of the legal and regulatory aspects surrounding IP rights, helping organizations develop and implement comprehensive IPR policies that safeguard their interests while promoting innovation. In addition to managing litigation matters, we provide litigation support services including expert witness testimony for IP licensing and related matters.

The firm is uniquely positioned to represent clients with collaborative patent licensing matters, including establishing and managing joint licensing programs and platforms. With a large network of technical advisors and specialty service providers, the firm is able to dedicate optimum resources and expertise to any project. Above all, we are dedicated to contributing to the success and growth of forward-thinking companies in the technology sector.
